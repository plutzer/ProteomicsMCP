TWO DISTINCT mTOR OUTPUT PATHWAYS
================================================================================

----------------------------
PATHWAY 1: TRANSLATION INITIATION (4EBP1/eIF4E)
----------------------------

mTOR → 4EBP1 phosphorylation → eIF4E release → Cap-dependent translation

Regulation: Controls which mRNAs are translated (cap-binding step)

Cancer-specific patterns:
- LUAD: Potential 4EBP1→proliferation link (r=0.97, trending)
- PDAC: 4EBP1 phosphorylated but NOT driving proliferation
- HNSCC/CCRCC: 4EBP1 phosphorylation DECREASED

----------------------------
PATHWAY 2: RIBOSOME ACTIVATION (S6K/RPS6)
----------------------------

mTOR → S6K phosphorylation → RPS6 phosphorylation → Ribosome activation

Regulation: Controls translation capacity and elongation efficiency

Cancer-specific patterns:
- PDAC: RPS6→proliferation (r=0.33, p=0.007) ✓ PRIMARY pathway
- LUAD: RPS6→proliferation (r=0.38, p=0.0009) ✓ PRIMARY pathway
- HNSCC: No RPS6→proliferation correlation

PDAC'S UNIQUE mTOR BIOLOGY
================================================================================

----------------------------
THE "EXCESS S6K CAPACITY" PARADOX IN PDAC
----------------------------

PDAC shows:
1. HIGH S2448 (normalized +0.26, p=0.007) → Excess S6K capacity
2. LOW ribosomes (RPS6 -0.79, p<0.0001) → Substrate deficit
3. INCREASED 4EBP1 phosphorylation → eIF4E available
4. RPS6→proliferation correlation (r=0.33, p=0.007) → Ribosome-dependent

**Interpretation:**

Despite having "excess S6K capacity" (high S2448 from low substrate demand),
PDAC's proliferation depends on RPS6 phosphorylation, NOT 4EBP1.

This means:
- S6K is not substrate-saturated (ribosomes are limiting)
- But proliferation still requires ribosome ACTIVATION (RPS6 phosphorylation)
- 4EBP1 is phosphorylated (eIF4E available) but doesn't drive proliferation
- Bottleneck is ribosome QUANTITY/ACTIVITY, not translation initiation

**Model:**

PDAC tumors operate with:
- Limited ribosomes (lost secretory phenotype)
- Active S6K that can phosphorylate available ribosomes
- Active mTOR→4EBP1 axis (eIF4E available)
- Proliferation gated by ribosome quantity/activity
- S6K has "excess capacity" only because ribosome substrate is so low

**Therapeutic Implications:**

1. mTOR inhibitors may work by reducing RPS6 phosphorylation
2. BUT 4EBP1 pathway is already active, so may not be main target
3. Consider S6K-specific inhibitors
4. Consider ribosome biogenesis inhibitors (further limit capacity)
5. Or target translation elongation (where ribosomes are rate-limiting)

COMPARISON TO OTHER CANCERS
----------------------------

**LUAD/LSCC/CCRCC (High Ribosome Saturation):**
- High ribosomes saturate S6K
- Low S2448 (no excess capacity)
- 4EBP1 phosphorylation DECREASED
- Proliferation driven by RPS6 (LUAD) or other mechanisms

**PDAC (Low Ribosome, Excess S6K):**
- Low ribosomes, S6K not saturated
- High S2448 (excess capacity relative to low substrate)
- 4EBP1 phosphorylation MAINTAINED/INCREASED
- Proliferation driven by RPS6 (ribosome-limited)

**Key Difference:**
- Other cancers: "Too much substrate, not enough enzyme"
- PDAC: "Too little substrate, enzyme waiting" but still substrate-limited

================================================================================
CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

1. **S2448 is NOT an mTOR activity marker:**
   - Functions as S6K capacity sensor (substrate competition)
   - Other MTOR sites show equal/better correlation with activity
   - Should NOT be used as primary activity biomarker

2. **Multiple candidate activity sites identified:**
   - S2481 (PSP: enzymatic activity induced, high confidence)
   - S2454 (r=0.931 with 4EBP1_T37)
   - S2478 (r=0.819 with 4EBP1_T37)
   - Require functional validation

3. **4EBP1 phosphorylation patterns are cancer-specific:**
   - Most cancers DECREASE 4EBP1 phosphorylation (HNSCC, CCRCC, LUAD)
   - PDAC INCREASES 4EBP1 phosphorylation (unique)
   - Suggests different translation control mechanisms

4. **Two distinct mTOR output pathways:**
   - RPS6 (ribosome activation): drives proliferation in LUAD, PDAC
   - 4EBP1 (translation initiation): potential role in LUAD, not PDAC
   - Cancer-specific pathway dependencies

5. **PDAC has unique mTOR biology:**
   - Ribosome-limited despite "excess S6K capacity"
   - Proliferation driven by RPS6, NOT 4EBP1
   - Requires ribosome-targeted therapies, not initiation-focused

6. **Therapeutic strategies must be pathway-matched:**
   - LUAD: Dual pathway targeting (RPS6 + 4EBP1)
   - PDAC: RPS6/ribosome-specific targeting
   - HNSCC: Alternative mechanisms (not translation)
   - CCRCC: Metabolic/angiogenic (current standard validated)

The field should move away from using S2448 as the primary mTOR activity
marker and adopt pathway-specific readouts (direct substrate phosphorylation)
matched to cancer-specific biology.
